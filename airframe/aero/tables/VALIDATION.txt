     This is the quantity that decides the lateral result: in the HL-20

     new airframe:  |Cn_da/Cl_da| <= 0.0374 (worst at alpha +6 deg)
     HL-20: |Cnda/Clda| = 0.36 .. 4.81
     yaw from full aileron (12 deg): 0.00399
     yaw from full rudder  (12 deg): 0.01383
  [PASS] rudder can counter full-throw aileron yaw: rudder authority is 3.5x what the aileron demands
     CAVEAT: the fuselage, boom and motor pod carry no vortex-lattice loading,
     so directional stability and rudder sidewash are both optimistic here.
